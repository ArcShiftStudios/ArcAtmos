# ArcAtmos

**Weather, brought to life.**

ArcAtmos is a modern weather application developed by **Arc Shift Studios**, built around a simple idea: weather information should be more than numbers, symbols, and repetitive forecast cards. It should feel alive, respond to the world around you, and present useful information through an experience that is as intuitive as it is visually engaging.

Combining atmospheric visuals, adaptive colors, smooth animations, and practical weather information, ArcAtmos aims to turn everyday weather checking into an immersive and personalized experience.

## 🌦️ Weather With Atmosphere

ArcAtmos is designed to make current weather conditions immediately understandable through a visually expressive interface. Instead of relying on a static layout for every forecast, the application aims to adapt its appearance to reflect the conditions outside.

Soft cloud formations, subtle atmospheric effects, carefully layered backgrounds, and weather-responsive visual elements help establish a distinctive identity while keeping the information readable.

From peaceful clear skies to cloudy afternoons, rainy evenings, and dramatic thunderstorms, the goal is to make the interface feel connected to the atmosphere it represents.

## 🎨 A Dynamic, Personalized Interface

ArcAtmos embraces a clean, modern design inspired by **Material Design 3**, combining expressive typography, balanced layouts, fluid transitions, and carefully selected colors.

The visual experience is designed to adapt to changing weather conditions and, where supported, complement the user's device wallpaper and system appearance. Weather-responsive backgrounds and subtle color transitions help the application feel cohesive without sacrificing clarity.

The result is an interface that feels personal, modern, and atmospheric rather than another generic weather dashboard.

## 📍 Weather Around You

ArcAtmos is designed to make local weather information easy to access, with location-aware functionality intended to help users check conditions relevant to where they are.

Depending on the available forecast data and implemented features, the application can present information such as:

- Current temperature and apparent temperature
- General weather conditions and descriptive summaries
- Daily high and low temperatures
- Hourly weather changes
- Upcoming forecasts
- Wind speed and direction
- Humidity and precipitation information

The focus is on presenting useful information in a clear hierarchy, helping users understand both current conditions and what to expect throughout the day.

## 🌫️ Air Quality Insights

Weather is only one part of understanding the environment. ArcAtmos also aims to incorporate air quality information, giving users additional context about the conditions around them.

Where supported by the selected data provider, this may include air quality index information, pollutant measurements, and related environmental indicators.

These details are intended to be presented in an accessible format, making environmental information easier to understand without overwhelming the main weather experience.

## ⛈️ Subtle, Living Weather Effects

ArcAtmos explores the use of restrained visual effects to make different weather conditions feel distinct.

Potential atmospheric details include softly animated clouds, gentle transitions between weather states, and occasional, carefully controlled lightning illumination during thunderstorms. These effects are intended to enhance the experience without becoming distracting or unnecessarily demanding on device resources.

The emphasis is on subtlety: animation should support the information rather than compete with it.

## ⚡ Designed for Everyday Use

A weather application should be quick to understand and comfortable to use every day. ArcAtmos prioritizes readable information, intuitive navigation, consistent spacing, and responsive interactions.

Its design philosophy balances visual ambition with practical usability, aiming to keep essential weather information accessible while allowing atmospheric visuals to provide character.

Performance and visual clarity remain important considerations, particularly on devices with limited resources.

## 🌐 Weather Data and Location

ArcAtmos is designed to integrate with weather data services to retrieve forecasts and atmospheric information. **Open-Meteo** is a potential data source for weather forecasts, with additional services considered where specific information, such as air quality, requires a separate provider.

Location-aware functionality is intended to help users access relevant local conditions, subject to the application's permissions and supported data sources.

Actual data availability, forecast coverage, API requirements, and supported features depend on the integrations included in each release.

## 🔒 Built With Purpose

ArcAtmos is part of the **Arc Shift Studios** ecosystem, a collection of projects focused on combining practical software with distinctive design and thoughtful user experiences.

The project aims to balance useful information, personalization, and visual expression while avoiding unnecessary clutter and excessive complexity.

The broader goal is to build an application that feels polished and enjoyable to use while remaining focused on its primary purpose: helping people understand the weather around them.

## 🚀 The Vision

ArcAtmos is built around the belief that utility and design do not need to be opposites. Weather information can be accurate and accessible while still feeling expressive, immersive, and connected to the environment it describes.

Whether checking the morning forecast, preparing for rain, or simply watching the atmosphere change throughout the day, ArcAtmos aims to make every glance at the weather feel a little more meaningful.
